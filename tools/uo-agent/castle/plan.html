How the draft is laid out

  • Every tile holds a stack of 4 chests, read from the top (z 18) down to the floor (z 6), like the tower's chest wall.
  • One chest per category, with the same bags and markers inside as in the tower. The tower squeezed 24 categories into 24 chests; the east wall has 60.
  • Gear sits at the north end, near the forge, then the valuables, then scrolls, reagents, gems and supplies.
  • All the potions are together at the south end of each wall: 2 chests per kind (3 for poison), since potions don't stack.
  • A free stack separates the stock from the potions, and free chests sit beside the gear, so things can grow without moving anything.
  • The forge gate. Your tables stand locked down across the east hall walkway at the forge. Behind them: the whole east wall, your vault and the east intake chests. In front: the forge chests, the smelting chests, the trash barrel and the spare backpacks, so anyone can use those. Clem releases one table to get through and always puts it back and locks it down, as with the south-east table now.
  • Secures. The 13 on the east wall stay as made on 18 Nov; your friend's 8 go back to plain locked-down chests (you, 19 Nov). Once the tables stand at the forge, the east wall wouldn't need its secures either; that's for later.
  • The west side, later. The west wall is free, unassigned and not secure; the west intake chests and your friend's row wait too. Only the east side is sorted for now.
  • Spare backpacks go to one chest by the forge (944,2327, floor), outside the tables, so emptied loot bags can be dropped there by anyone. Once it holds 125, further empty bags go in the trash.
